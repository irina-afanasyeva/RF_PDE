## Work presented

- Direct-fit results: a centered narrow Gaussian gives almost no benefit
  against the dense-LS RF baseline, despite supplying a mostly-novel

  by construction increased the coefficient norm but did not increase the
  local feature's relative contribution in the shock region.

  collocation points** ("increase weight to collocation points in the shock

  direct-fit plotting fix) and asked whether increasing the number of RF
  features might address it.
