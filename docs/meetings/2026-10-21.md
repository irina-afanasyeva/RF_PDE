# Meeting 09 — 2026-10-21

## Context

Following the RF basis-size work presented at the 2026-10-14 meeting
(`docs/meetings/2026-10-14.md`; `RESEARCH_LOG.md` Section 35), four
diagnostics were completed and reviewed at this meeting: random-seed
sensitivity (seeds 20–24 vs. canonical 42), a fixed Nx/Nt=50 ratio sweep
from `(50,1)` through `(3000,60)`, a learning-rate sensitivity sweep
(5e-4 canonical vs. 2e-4, 1e-4, 5e-5), and a Nx=500,Nt=10 longer-training
diagnostic (1000/2000/5000 epochs) — see `RESEARCH_LOG.md` Sections 36–39.

## Work presented

- Seed sensitivity: seed 42 is not an unusually poor realization among
  seeds 20–24 and 42; genuine seed-to-seed variability is substantial.
- Fixed Nx/Nt=50 ratio sweep: non-monotonic error vs. coefficient count,
  no systematic improvement from increasing capacity beyond the canonical
  scale; the two largest planned configurations were not run.
- Learning-rate sweep: smaller learning rates are worse than canonical at
  a fixed 1000-epoch budget, but all are still improving substantially,
  not converged.
- Longer-training diagnostic: restoring the original 5000-epoch training
  duration (under a necessarily re-initialized optimizer, since no
  optimizer state was ever saved) gives only a small global/smooth
  improvement and no shock-region improvement, even as PDE loss keeps
  decreasing.
