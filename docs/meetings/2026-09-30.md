## Work presented

- Shock-region weighting result: no activation; accuracy worsened at high
  alpha (up to 30% effective objective share tested).
- Simple temporal (`d*t*G(x)`) result: no activation; reducing the temporal
  representation from 10 random features to one scalar changed almost
  nothing.
- Follow-up direct-fit diagnostic: searching over center and width (not
  just `center=0`), with the analytically optimal amplitude, a **shifted**
  Gaussian (`c~.01`, `sigma~.0055`, negative amplitude) recovers a
  substantial fraction of the direct-fit RF residual (~17.8% global, ~27.5%
  shock-region error reduction) — this refines, rather than contradicts,
  the earlier centered-Gaussian conclusion.
